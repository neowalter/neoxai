### [FieldTrip 置换检验改为把「并列」计入 p 值（>= 而非 >）](https://github.com/fieldtrip/fieldtrip/pull/2608)
*FieldTrip Now Counts Ties in Monte Carlo p-Values (>= Instead of >)*

**简介：** FieldTrip 合并 #2608（修 #2607）：`ft_statistics_montecarlo` 与 `clusterstat` 改用 **`>=`** 比较观测统计量与随机化分布。原因是此前用严格不等式，**与观测值恰好相等的随机化不被计数**；而当 `cfg.numrandomization='all'` 时，**恒等置换本身就是一个并列**——漏掉它就等于少加一个 `+1`，p 值系统性地偏小（在边界显著性上尤其致命：0.049 与 0.051 的差别足以改变结论）。这是置换检验最经典的陷阱，MNE 上个月也刚修过同类问题。对做 cluster-based 置换统计的团队，建议升级后重跑关键对比；若你的结果恰好在 0.05 附近，务必重新核对。

**Brief:** FieldTrip merged #2608 (fixing #2607) so ft_statistics_montecarlo and clusterstat compare with >= instead of strict inequalities. Randomisations exactly equal to the observed statistic were not counted, and with numrandomization='all' the identity permutation is such a tie — omitting it equals dropping a +1, making p systematically too small right at the 0.05 boundary.

- #2608：`ft_statistics_montecarlo` 与 `clusterstat` 此前用**严格不等式**比较观测统计量与随机化分布，于是**与观测值完全相等的随机化没有被计数**。
- 当 `cfg.numrandomization='all'` 时，**恒等置换本身就是一个并列**，漏计它就等于少加了一个 `+1`——p 值系统性偏小。
- 这是置换检验的经典陷阱：正确的 p 值应包含「至少一样极端」的随机化（`>=`），而非只数「更极端」的（`>`）。

- #2608 fixes ft_statistics_montecarlo and clusterstat comparing the observed statistic to the randomisation distribution with strict inequalities, so randomisations exactly equal to the observed value were not counted.
- With cfg.numrandomization='all' the identity permutation is such a tie, and missing it is equivalent to omitting a +1 — the p-value is systematically too small.
- This is the classic permutation-test trap: p should count randomisations at least as extreme (>=), not strictly more extreme (>).
