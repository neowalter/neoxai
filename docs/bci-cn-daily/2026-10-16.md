**Brief:** MOABB merged #1273, excluding Liu2024 subjects 5, 15 and 49 by default: EDF sub-05 is byte-identical to subject 4's sub-04, and 15/49 are reprocessed copies of subjects 11/47 — in sourcedata.zip each equals its partner's raw recording plus i.i.d. uniform noise in [0,1000) uV per row, though participants.tsv calls them different patients. Keeping them leaks one recording across splits.

- #1273：Liu2024 的被试 5、15、49 由被试 4、11、47 的录制派生——EDF sub-05 与 sub-04 **逐字节相同**（15/49 是 11/47 的重处理副本）。
- 在 sourcedata.zip 中，这些副本等于其对应原始录制在每一行上叠加 i.i.d. 均匀噪声 [0, 1000) µV；而 participants.tsv 却把它们列为不同患者。
- 后果与处理：保留它们会让同一被试的 EEG 以两个 ID 出现在训练与测试两侧（交叉验证泄漏）；MOABB 现**默认排除**这三个 subject。

- #1273: Liu2024 subjects 5, 15 and 49 are derived from subjects 4, 11 and 47 — EDF sub-05 is byte-identical to sub-04, and 15/49 are reprocessed copies of 11/47.
- In sourcedata.zip those copies equal their partner's raw recording plus i.i.d. uniform noise in [0, 1000) uV on every row, yet participants.tsv lists them as different patients.
- Impact and handling: keeping them puts one subject's EEG under two IDs on both sides of a split (cross-validation leakage), so MOABB now excludes the three subjects by default.
