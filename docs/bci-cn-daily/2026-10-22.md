**简介：** BrainFlow 合并 #867，修掉一个会静默放大信号的设备级错误：**NeuroPawn Knight 与 Knight IMU 的 EEG 采样此前用运行期 `float` 做缩放，漏掉了 79.57 这个除数**，现在两个读取器统一使用 `KnightBase` 上的编译期常量 `4.0/(32768-1)/12.0/79.57*1e6`（µV）。少除一个系数意味着同一段信号读出来的微伏值被系统性放大约 80 倍——不报错，但频带功率、阈值判断、伪迹与阻抗判定全部随之偏移。对用该硬件做过实验的团队，建议核对记录里的幅度量级（正常 EEG 多为 10–100 µV），必要时按正确系数重标定后重跑分析。BrainFlow 是跨设备统一采集库，这类修复也提醒：换设备前先做幅度合理性检查。

**Brief:** BrainFlow merged #867 fixing a silently amplified signal: NeuroPawn Knight and Knight IMU EEG samples were scaled with a runtime float that omitted the 79.57 divisor, and both readers now use one compile-time constant (4.0/(32768-1)/12.0/79.57*1e6 uV). Missing a divisor scales readings up nearly 80x, quietly shifting band power, thresholds and artefact checks.

- #867：Knight 与 Knight IMU 的 EEG 样本此前用运行期 `float` 缩放，**漏掉了 79.57 这个除数**；现在两个 reader 统一使用 `KnightBase` 上的编译期常量。

- The correct factor is 4.0 / (32768-1) / 12.0 / 79.57 * 1e6 in microvolts — without the divisor the same signal reads almost 80x too large.
- Amplitude errors quietly distort band power, thresholds and impedance/artefact checks; teams that used this hardware should re-run affected analyses.

### [EEGDash 修并发刷新时共享 sidecar 被覆盖，并让头部实测值优先于 sidecar](https://github.com/eegdash/EEGDash/pull/490)
*EEGDash Stops Shared Sidecars Being Clobbered During Concurrent Refresh and Prefers Header Values*

**简介：** EEGDash 在 10 月 21 日继续加固 ingest：#490 修下载器的**并发竞态**——多个刷新任务同时写同一个共享 sidecar 时会互相覆盖，现在会保留共享文件；#491 把 digest 的取值优先级改为「**EDF 头部实测的采样率与记录数优先于 sidecar 里的近似值**」，此前 sidecar 的近似值会盖掉实测值，造成时长与采样率偏差。这两项都不是新功能，却决定入库数值对不对：采样率错一档，整个频率轴都错；记录数错一位，时间轴整体偏移且很难察觉。对依赖 EEGDash 元数据做批量分析的团队，升级后建议抽样比对几个数据集的头部与下载文件是否一致。

**Brief:** EEGDash kept hardening ingestion on Oct 21: #490 fixes a downloader race where concurrent refreshes overwrote shared sidecar files, and #491 makes digest prefer measured EDF header sampling rate and record count over sidecar approximations. Neither is a feature, but both decide whether ingested numbers are right: a wrong rate skews the frequency axis, an off-by-one count shifts time.

- #490：并发刷新时共享 sidecar 文件可能被互相覆盖（下载器的竞态），现改为保留共享 sidecar。
- #491：digest 改为「EDF 头部采样率与记录数优先于 sidecar 值」——此前 sidecar 里的近似值会覆盖实测值，导致时长/采样率偏差。
- 这两项直接影响批量入库的数值正确性：采样率错一档会让频率轴全错，记录数错一位会让时间轴偏移。

- #490: shared sidecar files could be overwritten by concurrent refreshes (a downloader race), now preserved across them.
- #491: digest now prefers EDF header sampling rate and record count over sidecar values, which previously could override measurements with approximations.
- Both affect numeric correctness of bulk ingestion: a wrong sampling rate skews the whole frequency axis, an off-by-one record count shifts the time axis.
