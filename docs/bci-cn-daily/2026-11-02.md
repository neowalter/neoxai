**简介：** EEGDash 合并 #511：**标记「MNE 读出的量级不合理」的通道**，并修正以 **nV/pV** 声明单位的 EDF 头部缩放。EEG 数据里单位声明错误是经典陷阱——把 nV 当 µV 会让幅度差 **1000 倍**，伪迹阈值、频带功率、跨数据集归一化全部被带偏，而代码不会报任何错。这个 PR 的处理方式值得借鉴：**先标记、不静默修正**，让人决定是否重标定，避免把「可能是记录问题」的数据悄悄改成「看起来正常」。对做大规模数据入库或跨数据集元分析的团队，建议在流水线里消费这些标记并统计出现频率。

**Brief:** EEGDash merged #511 flagging channels MNE reads at an implausible scale and correcting EDF headers that declare nV or pV units. Misdeclared units are a classic EEG trap — treating nV as µV shifts amplitudes by 1000x and skews artefact thresholds, band power and cross-dataset normalisation with no error raised. Flagging instead of silently rescaling keeps a human in the loop.

- #511：对 **MNE 读出的量级明显不合理**的通道打标记，并修正以 **nV/pV** 声明单位的 EDF 头部缩放。
- 单位声明错（把 nV 当 µV）会让幅度差 1000 倍，进而破坏伪迹阈值、频带功率与跨数据集比较——且不会报错。
- 对入库/分析流水线，这类「标记而不是静默修正」的做法更安全：先暴露、再决定是否重标定。

- #511 flags channels that MNE reads at an implausible scale and corrects EDF headers declaring nV or pV units.
- A wrong unit declaration (treating nV as µV) shifts amplitudes by 1000x, breaking artefact thresholds, band power and cross-dataset comparisons without any error.
- Flagging rather than silently rescaling is the safer choice for ingestion pipelines: surface it, then decide whether to recalibrate.
