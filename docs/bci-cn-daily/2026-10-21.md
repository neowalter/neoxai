### [解码前必须避开的坑：坏电极、试次不平衡与降采样权衡](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1XenF6rEXi/)
*MVPA Pitfalls to Avoid: Bad Electrodes, Unbalanced Trials and Downsampling Trade-offs*

**简介：** B站「BrainScience」发布的解码方法讲解（09:24），把 MVPA 里最容易踩的三个坑讲得很具体：①**坏电极与伪迹会自己制造「显著」**——用 32 个电极区分两条件时，一个坏电极就足以把 A/B 分开，但这不反映条件差异；若 A 条件眨眼多、B 少，准确率虚高也只是眨眼差异；②**条件间试次数要平衡**（A 100 vs B 30 会扭曲分类权重，常规做法是从 A 随机抽 30，且只在被试内平衡）；③**信噪比权衡**——降采样到 250 Hz 计算量只有 1000 Hz 的四分之一而结果差异不大，super-trial（每 4 个平均）提高信噪比但减少样本量。对刚上手的解码分析者，这三条比任何模型选择都更早决定结果可信度。

**Brief:** A Chinese decoding tutorial spells out three MVPA pitfalls: bad electrodes or artefacts can manufacture significance (one bad electrode suffices to 'separate' two conditions, and unequal blink rates inflate accuracy), trial counts must be balanced within subject (A 100 vs B 30 skews weights; subsample A to 30), and SNR matters (250 Hz costs a quarter of 1000 Hz; super-trials trade n for SNR).

- **坏电极会自己「分类成功」**：用 32 个电极区分 A/B 时，一个坏电极就足以把两类分开，但差异来自电极而非条件；伪迹同理（A 条件眨眼多、B 少，准确率虚高只是眨眼差异）。
- **条件间试次必须平衡**：A 有 100 个、B 只有 30 个会影响分类权重，常规做法是从 A 随机抽 30 个；注意这是被试内平衡，不同被试之间可以不等。
- **信噪比权衡**：降采样（1000 Hz 是 250 Hz 的 4 倍计算量，而 200 Hz 与原始差异不大）与 super-trial（每 4 个试次平均换来更高信噪比但样本量减少）。

- Bad electrodes can 'classify' on their own: with 32 electrodes distinguishing A from B, one bad electrode alone can separate the classes even though the difference is artefactual; same for artefacts when one condition has more blinks.
- Trial counts must be balanced across conditions: A with 100 trials versus B with 30 skews classifier weights, so the usual fix is randomly subsampling A to 30; the balance is within-subject, not across subjects.
- Signal-to-noise trade-offs: downsampling (1000 Hz costs 4x the compute of 250 Hz while 200 Hz differs little from raw) and super-trials (averaging every four trials raises SNR but shrinks sample size).
