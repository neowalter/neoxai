### [EEGLAB 修 ANOVA 单精度累积：F 偏差 0.6%，p 值在 6e-8 以下被算成 0](https://github.com/sccn/eeglab/pull/977)
*EEGLAB Fixes Single-Precision ANOVA: F Off by 0.6%, p-Values Saturating to Zero*

**简介：** EEGLAB 合并 #977，修掉一个会静默影响统计结论的数值问题：ANOVA 辅助函数把 double 输入累积在**单精度**中，而平方和本身是抵消式运算（ssA=expA−expT、ssAS=expAS−expA−expS+expT 等），单精度约 7 位有效数字会把共同量级抹掉。结果是 F 值相对偏差约 0.6%，并且 `p = 1-fcdf(F)` 在一阶 F 分布尾部被截断为 0（约 6e-8 以下）——对做 ERP 组间比较、需要多重比较校正的研究，这等于把「不显著」误判成「极端显著」。修复改为双精度累积，并顺带修了 nonpositive F 的返回值、rm_anova2 的上尾计算与尾概率共享。凡是在 EEGLAB 里跑过 ANOVA 的项目，建议升级后重跑关键统计。

**Brief:** EEGLAB merged #977 fixing a silent statistics bug: ANOVA helpers accumulated double inputs in single precision while sums of squares are cancellation terms, erasing the shared magnitude. F was off by ~0.6% and p = 1-fcdf(F) saturated to 0 below about 6e-8, turning non-significance into extreme significance in ERP comparisons; the fix accumulates in double.

- 根因：ANOVA 辅助函数把 double 输入累积在**单精度**里，而平方和是抵消式运算（ssA=expA−expT 等），约 7 位有效数字吃掉了共同量级。
- 后果：F 值相对误差约 0.6%；更严重的是 p=1−fcdf(F) 在一阶 F 分布尾部**饱和为 0**（约 6e-8 以下），多重比较与校正会因此失效。
- 修复：改为双精度累积；另修 nonpositive F 的 p 值返回、rm_anova2 改用 F 上尾、共享尾概率并去掉 Cephes 例外（8 个文件，PR 由 AI 编码 agent 提交）。

- Root cause: the ANOVA helpers accumulated double inputs in single precision, while sums of squares are cancellation terms (ssA = expA - expT, and so on), so about seven significant digits ate the shared magnitude.
- Impact: F was off by roughly 0.6%, and worse, p = 1-fcdf(F) saturated to 0 in the first-order tail (below about 6e-8), breaking multiple-comparison correction.
- Fix: accumulate in double; also return p-values for nonpositive F, use the F upper tail in rm_anova2, share the tail and drop the Cephes allowance (8 files; the PR was submitted by an AI coding agent).
