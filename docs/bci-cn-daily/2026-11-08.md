**简介：** Eelbrain 合并 #275：新增 **`RawICA`** 选项，允许**按任务分别估计 ICA**，而不是在整段连续记录上做一次全局分解。这个改动的动机很实际：不同任务之间的伪迹构成与状态差异明显（静息 vs 任务、不同刺激条件），全局 ICA 经常在某一任务上清理得很好、另一任务却残留眨眼或肌电——而使用者往往只检查了第一个任务。代价也要清楚：每个任务各自拟合会增加计算量，且**分量编号在不同任务间不可直接比较**，做跨任务成分对齐时需自己处理。对做多任务范式的团队，建议先在小数据集上比较「全局 vs 按任务」的伪迹残留与分量稳定性，再决定是否切换。

**Brief:** Eelbrain merged #275 adding a RawICA option to estimate ICA per task rather than once over a continuous recording. Different tasks have different artefact composition and states, so a global decomposition often cleans one task well and leaves blinks or EMG in another. The cost is one fit per task and non-comparable component numbering; compare residual artefacts before switching.

- #275：新增 **`RawICA`** 选项，允许**按任务分别估计 ICA**，而不是在整段连续记录上只做一次分解。

- 代价是每个任务各自拟合，计算量与分量编号的可比性需注意；跨任务比较成分时要自行对齐。

- #275 adds a RawICA option letting ICA be estimated per task instead of once over the whole continuous recording.
- The motivation is practical: artefact composition and state differ across tasks (rest versus task, different stimulus conditions), so a global ICA often cleans one task well and leaves residues in another.
- The cost is one fit per task, with more compute and non-comparable component numbering; align components yourself before cross-task comparison.
